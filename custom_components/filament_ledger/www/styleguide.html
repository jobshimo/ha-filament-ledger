Filament Ledger — Styleguide

Every surface the panel renders, driven by the panel's own render methods. No Home Assistant, no websocket, no real data.

This page imports STYLES from filament-ledger-panel.js and adopts that exact stylesheet into each specimen's shadow root, then asks a real, unmounted <filament-ledger-panel> instance to render each view from fixtures. There is no second copy of the markup and no second copy of the CSS, so this catalogue cannot drift away from the panel.

Specimens are resizable — drag the bottom-right corner of any frame to check a component against a narrower container. See docs/16-visual-system.md §16.4.
